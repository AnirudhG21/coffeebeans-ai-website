Business Problem

DSD operates as a premier provider of advanced inflight entertainment (IFE) solutions across Southeast Asia, especially dominant in Indonesia with over 76% market share. The inflight entertainment industry faces unique challenges including the shift from legacy web-based systems to app-based platforms, ensuring content variety and freshness, managing data collection without internet connectivity onboard, and monetizing an advertising-driven revenue model in a fiercely cost-sensitive aviation environment. Airlines rely on capturing passenger attention through engaging content despite short flight durations and limited connectivity, while controlling costs and complying with regulatory standards such as GDPR.
